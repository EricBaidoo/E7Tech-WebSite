**Panel Composition:**

- 📊 Sarah Osei — Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist

---

## Executive Summary

The E7 website has strong bones. The brand identity is well-defined, and the core messaging
(Trusted Technology, Intelligent Transformation) is clear. However, there are critical conversion
gaps, inconsistent quality across pages, and several dead-end stub pages that actively damage credibility.

**Overall Marketability Score: 6.2 / 10**

---

## Page-by-Page Audit

### 1. Homepage (index.php) — Score: 7/10
| Category | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | 7-slide auto-rotating carousel dilutes focus; users miss key messages | HIGH |
| Value Prop | "Three platforms. Infinite possibilities." is vague. No specific outcomes stated | MEDIUM |
| Social Proof | Logo strip is solid; but no client count, years, or case study link | MEDIUM |
| CTA | Final CTA is weak: "Consult a Lead Architect" — too jargon-heavy | MEDIUM |
| Missing | No pricing signal, no free consultation hook, no trust badges | HIGH |

Panel Verdict: Replace the 7-slide carousel with a single bold static hero. Add a secondary CTA
like "Book a Free 30-Min Strategy Call" next to every primary button.

---

### 2. EduCore (products/educore.php) — Score: 8.5/10 ★ BEST PAGE
| Category | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Strong — cinematic, clear headline, clear CTA | PASS |
| Flow | Excellent: Hero → Social Proof → Ecosystem → Deep Dives → Trust → CTA | PASS |
| Objection Handling | "Transition with Confidence" section is gold — rare and powerful | PASS |
| Testimonial | Present. Needs star rating (★★★★★) and company logo added | MEDIUM |
| Pricing | No pricing anchor. "Starting from GHS X/term" hint would reduce friction | MEDIUM |
| Missing | No video demo embed or "Watch 2-min Overview" button | MEDIUM |

Panel Verdict: Strongest page on the site. Minor additions (star rating, pricing anchor, video)
will push this to 9.5/10.

---

### 3. FaithOS (products/faithos.php) — Score: 6/10
| Category | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Has hero and CTA but structurally weaker than EduCore | MEDIUM |
| Consistency | Does NOT have the same cinematic redesign as EduCore — looks like a different site | HIGH |
| Features | Modules listed but no compelling "why" narrative — just a list | MEDIUM |
| Social Proof | No testimonial, no logo strip for churches/ministries | HIGH |
| Targeting | No distinction between small church vs large ministry | MEDIUM |

Panel Verdict: FaithOS looks like version 1 while EduCore is version 3. Must be redesigned
with the same cinematic approach used for EduCore to maintain brand consistency.

---

### 4. FraudDetect / LMS / ZeroTrust / Luke7 — Score: 3/10 *** CRITICAL ***
| Category | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Content | These are PLACEHOLDER/STUB pages. FraudDetect says "content is being designed" | CRITICAL |
| Credibility | Prospects clicking these will bounce and lose all trust in the brand | CRITICAL |
| Brand Damage | Stub pages signal the company is not ready, undermining premium positioning | CRITICAL |

Panel Verdict: These pages must either be given real content immediately OR removed from all
navigation. A stub page is WORSE than no page. Replace with "Coming Soon + Email Capture".

## TOP 5 PRIORITY ACTION ITEMS

1. [CRITICAL] Kill or Complete the Stub Pages (FraudDetect, LMS, ZeroTrust, Luke7)
   → Replace with "Coming Soon + Email Capture" page immediately.

2. [HIGH] Redesign FaithOS with Cinematic EduCore Aesthetic
   → Brand inconsistency signals disorganization.

3. [HIGH] Replace Homepage 7-Slide Carousel with Single High-Impact Hero
   → Carousels are a conversion killer. One message, one CTA converts better.

4. [MEDIUM] Add a Pricing Anchor to EduCore and FaithOS
   → "Starting from GHS X" removes the biggest barrier (pricing uncertainty).

5. [MEDIUM] Add Social Proof Numbers to Homepage and About Page
   → "Trusted by 50+ organizations" is more powerful than any headline.
