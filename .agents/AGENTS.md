# E7 Technology Solutions - Design Aesthetic & UX Philosophy

When writing HTML/CSS or using Tailwind CSS for E7 Technology Solutions, strictly adhere to the following design philosophy modeled after Google and Microsoft:

## 1. Visual-First Communication ("Self-Communicative")
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Rely heavily on high-quality graphics, icons, product mockups, and visual diagrams to explain concepts. 
- **Less Text, More Impact:** Avoid "walls of text" and long write-ups. Keep copy extremely concise (just strong headlines and short 1-2 sentence subtext). Let the visuals do the talking.

## 2. Google/Microsoft Corporate Aesthetic
- **Minimalist & Bright:** Use lots of white space, clean white or very light gray backgrounds (like `#FAFAFA`). Avoid heavy dark modes, neon glows, and excessive glassmorphism.
- **Subtle Depth:** Use soft, flat, and subtle box-shadows rather than intense glowing effects.
- **Typography:** Use clean, highly legible sans-serif fonts (like Inter). Keep typography straightforward, flat, and high-contrast (dark gray/navy on white).
- **Corporate Accents:** Use the brand cyan/blue colors sparingly as precise accents (e.g., button backgrounds, icon colors, active link states, progress bars) rather than massive background gradients.
